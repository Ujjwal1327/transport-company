How do we move your belongings?

Packing: This is the first step where packers and movers pack your goods with materials such as bubble wrap, cardboard, and paper for safe transport.

Loading: Once your belongings are packed, they are safely and securely loaded onto trucks to avoid any unnecessary movement that could cause damage.

Transporting: After loading, the NextGen transports your goods to your chosen location using safe and secure roadways.

Unloading: This is the time when your goods have arrived at your destination. The packers and movers will unload them from the vehicle and help you put them in the designated area of your new home or office.

Unpacking: Once all your items are unloaded, they must be unpacked in the presence of the delivery guy so you can claim for any damages that may have occurred during transit.

How to get the best of house shifting?

step1

Get a quotation.

Choose 4-5 reliable transportation companies, such as NextGen Safe N Secure, Gati Transport, Aggarwal Transport, ABC Transport, or any other best in your city. Get quotations from all the companies. To get an exact quotation, you’ll need to provide details about your household goods. Providing all these details will help you get a better price and protection of your goods.

step2

Choose a reliable company

Choose a reliable company with proper GST registration and company registration with the government. Then, look at their Google reviews, Sulekha reviews, and all other reviews to make an informed decision. Choose the company that not only provides the best rate but also gives you the best customer experience.

step3

Booking

After finding a reliable house shifting company, provide the transporter with all the details about your house, including details about any special items. If they agree with all the conditions, schedule the pickup.

step4

Arrange your household stuff.

Properly arranging your items before the loading begins will streamline the entire process, reduce time, and ensure that the goods are loaded efficiently, safely, and without unnecessary delays.

step5

Tracking

Once your household goods are loaded, you’ll get a tracking ID to monitor its movements. If you notice anything off, such as the parcel not moving as per the schedule, contact the transporter directly and inquire about the issue and the estimated time for resolution.

step6

Delivery

When your goods are delivered, always check them carefully. Make sure everything is just as it was when loaded. Inspect all items in the presence of the transporter. This step is important — it helps with the claim process if any damage occurred in transit.

Factors that can affect the cost of car shipping

Distance

The longer the distance, the higher the cost for transportation.

Terrain also plays a major role in determining the cost; for example, shifting your house to Srinagar is more costly because they have less rail infrastructure and hard commuting routes.

Volume and Weight of Belongings:

The amount of items you’re transporting is a factor.

Heavier items require more manpower, which increases the cost. Therefore, remove any unnecessary items.

Accessibility

Elevators: Availability of elevators, the number of floors, and the distance from the parking area can affect the loading process and the overall cost for household shifting.

Narrow or Difficult Passage: These kinds of routes require additional labor, leading to an increase in the overall cost for household shifting.

Type of move

Local HouseholdShifting: It is generally cost-effective.

Interstate HouseholdShifting: More complex and requires professional handling, and costs much higher than local shifting.

Packing

Normal packaging: This kind of packaging is designed to withstand normal wear and tear.

Advanced packaging: Special packaging, usually for fragile items like TVs or electronics

Time of Shifting

Peak Season: Vacation or summer can lead to higher prices due to increased demand.

Difficult Weather: Shifting during the monsoon can be expensive, as it requires additional protection for goods.

Origin and Final Point
